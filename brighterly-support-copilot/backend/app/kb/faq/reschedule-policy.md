title: Rescheduling or cancelling a lesson
---
Lessons last 45 minutes. You can reschedule or cancel a lesson free of charge if it starts more than 6 hours from now. Inside 6 hours the lesson is treated as late: it cannot be moved or cancelled for free and the lesson is deducted from the balance. You can ask support to review an exceptional case. To reschedule, open Lessons, choose the lesson and pick a new time, or ask the assistant to propose available slots.
